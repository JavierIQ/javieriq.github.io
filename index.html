About Me

I am an Android Developer and Software Engineer with over 6 years of experience in technology and 4 years developing mobile applications. My expertise lies in building Android apps using Kotlin, and I am proficient with modern development tools and frameworks such as Jetpack Compose and XML views.

Throughout my career, I have integrated various technologies to enhance the functionality and performance of the applications I have worked on. I have extensive experience working with REST APIs, GraphQL, and Firebase databases, ensuring seamless data management and real-time updates within the apps.

My professional journey includes over 2 years of experience working with Java and the Spring framework. This has provided me with a solid foundation in backend development and a comprehensive understanding of full-stack application architecture.

Something more personal is that I like art, cinema and reading about science, authors like Siddhartha Mukherjee and Carl Sagan and philosophy, to always know something more about this world.
